# Equipment Guide

The goal of ReForge is not to build an expensive home gym.

Instead, the objective is to build a small collection of equipment that allows nearly every rehabilitation exercise to be performed safely.

Every piece of equipment in this chapter has been selected because it provides the greatest rehabilitation value for the lowest cost.

# 1. Resistance Tube Band Set

## Why It Is Important

This is the single most useful rehabilitation tool.

One band set can replace dozens of gym machines while allowing smooth, joint-friendly resistance.

For unstable shoulders, elastic resistance is often safer than free weights because resistance gradually increases rather than loading the shoulder heavily from the start.

---

## Used For

- External Rotation
- Internal Rotation
- Face Pulls
- Serratus Punch
- Band Rows
- Pallof Press
- Triceps Extensions
- Biceps Curl
- Scapular Retraction

---

## Buy

A complete set with:

- Five resistance levels
- Handles
- Door anchor
- Ankle straps
- Carry bag

---

# 2. Door Anchor

The door anchor converts almost any door into a cable machine.

Always use a strong, closed door.

Pull against the side where the door closes toward you.

Never attach the band to a weak door.

---

# 3. Exercise Mat

Provides comfort during floor exercises.

Used for:

- Dead Bug
- Bird Dog
- Side Plank
- Supine Serratus Punch
- Stretching
- Mobility work

---

# 4. Mini Loop Bands

Useful for lower-body activation and shoulder stability drills.

Exercises include:

- Lateral Walks
- Monster Walks
- Glute Bridge
- Shoulder Activation

---

# 5. Foam Roller

Used for mobility and posture.

Not for strengthening.

Useful before or after workouts.

Exercises include:

- Thoracic Extensions
- Pec Release
- Lat Release

---

# Optional Equipment

## Adjustable Bench

Useful for:

- Incline Push-up Progressions
- Chest Supported Row
- Supported Exercises

---

## Dumbbells

Start light.

For shoulder rehabilitation:

2–5 kg is usually sufficient.

Heavy weights are unnecessary during Phase 1.

---

## Pull-Up Bar

Not recommended during early rehabilitation.

Useful only during later strength phases.

---

## Kettlebell

Mainly for lower body.

Examples:

- Goblet Squat
- Romanian Deadlift
- Farmer Carry (later phases only)

---

# Equipment Safety

Inspect bands regularly.

Replace cracked or damaged bands immediately.

Always anchor resistance bands securely.

Never allow bands to snap back toward your face.

Wear stable shoes during standing exercises.

Maintain a clear exercise area.

Quality of movement is always more important than resistance.
