# Introduction

> "Rebuild your body. Understand your recovery."

Welcome to **ReForge**.

ReForge is a rehabilitation operating system designed to guide recovery through structured exercise, education, and consistent habit building.

Unlike traditional workout programs that simply prescribe exercises, ReForge explains **why** each exercise matters, **how** to perform it safely, and **when** to progress.

The goal is not simply to complete workouts.

The goal is to build a stronger, more stable, and more resilient body while understanding the rehabilitation process.

---

## Who This Handbook Is For

This handbook is intended for people who:

- Are recovering from shoulder instability or injury.
- Want a structured rehabilitation program.
- Need a clear home or gym routine.
- Want to understand the purpose behind every exercise.
- Prefer evidence-informed rehabilitation over random online advice.

---

## What Makes ReForge Different

ReForge combines four elements into one system:

- A structured rehabilitation program.
- An educational exercise library.
- Progress tracking and recovery monitoring.
- Intelligent guidance based on your recovery status.

Each exercise is designed to teach as well as strengthen.

---

## Our Philosophy

Every exercise should answer five questions:

1. What is this exercise?
2. Why am I doing it?
3. How do I perform it correctly?
4. What mistakes should I avoid?
5. How do I know I'm ready to progress?

If those questions are answered, rehabilitation becomes more understandable and more consistent.

---

## A Living Handbook

This handbook is intended to evolve.

As ReForge grows, new exercises, educational material, and rehabilitation programs will be added while maintaining a consistent structure.

The handbook serves as the single source of truth for the ReForge website, mobile application, printable guides, and future AI rehabilitation assistant.
