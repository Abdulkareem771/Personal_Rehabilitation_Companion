# Videos

Video files will be organized by purpose, not by body region.

## Subfolders

- [Exercise Loops](./exercise-loops/README.md)
- [Coaching](./coaching/README.md)

## General Rules

- Exercise loop videos are silent and short.
- Coaching videos may include speech and captions.
- Filenames must follow [Naming Conventions](../naming-conventions.md).
- Video quality must follow [Video Standards](../video-standards.md).
