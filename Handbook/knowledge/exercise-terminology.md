# Exercise Terminology

This document standardizes prescription language across exercises and programs.

## Set

A set is a group of repetitions or a continuous time block completed before resting.

## Rep

A rep is one complete movement cycle or one prescribed hold.

## Hold

A hold is a sustained position or contraction for a specified time.

## Rest

Rest is the planned recovery time between sets or exercises.

Rest should be long enough to preserve the clinical goal. Short rest increases fatigue. Longer rest improves quality and strength output.

## Frequency

Frequency describes how often the exercise or program is performed.

Examples:

- `3 days per week`
- `daily`
- `2-3 sessions per week`

## Intensity

Intensity describes difficulty. It can include resistance, effort, speed, position, range, or complexity.

Use simple terms when possible:

- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Symptom-led

## Volume

Volume is the total amount of work.

Examples:

- Sets multiplied by reps.
- Total hold time.
- Total weekly sessions.
- Total sport exposures.

## Range

Range describes how far the movement travels.

Range may be:

- Full.
- Partial.
- Comfortable.
- Pain-free.
- Symptom-limited.

## Quality

Quality describes how well the movement matches the intended pattern.

Quality includes:

- Control.
- Alignment.
- Smoothness.
- Breathing.
- Lack of compensation.
