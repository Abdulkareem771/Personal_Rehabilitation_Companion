# Desk Worker

## Objective

Offset prolonged sitting and keyboard posture with brief mobility breaks and two weekly strengthening sessions.

## Duration

- **Typical length:** 6 weeks before reassessment.
- **Frequency:** Daily micro-breaks plus 2 strength sessions per week.
- **Session length:** 3-5 minutes for breaks, 20-30 minutes for strength sessions.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Exercise Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Strength A | Mobility, wall slide, pull-apart, core |
| Tuesday | Micro-breaks | Thoracic extension and rotation |
| Wednesday | Micro-breaks | Thoracic extension and serratus |
| Thursday | Strength B | Mobility, prone work, face pull, core |
| Friday | Micro-breaks | Thoracic mobility |
| Saturday | Optional reset | Daily posture routine |
| Sunday | Rest | Weekly review |

## Progression Rules

Progress when:

- Micro-breaks are completed on at least 4 workdays per week.
- Strength sessions do not create next-day neck or shoulder symptoms.
- Pulling and prone work remain controlled through the final reps.

## Regression Rules

Regress when:

- Replace Strength Session B with the micro-break menu if fatigue is high.
- Reduce face pull resistance if neck tension appears.
- Use only thoracic mobility for 24-48 hours if symptoms flare.

## Clinical Checkpoints

- **Pain:** Symptoms do not increase across the workweek.
- **Work tolerance:** Sitting tolerance improves or stays stable.
- **Control:** Scapular work feels muscular between the shoulder blades, not mainly in the neck.
- **Adherence:** Breaks are easy enough to repeat without disrupting work.

## Completion Criteria

The 6-week block is complete when the person completes at least 80% of planned strength sessions and reports more consistent desk tolerance without symptom escalation.
