# Daily Posture

## Objective

Create a short daily routine that restores thoracic movement, reinforces scapular control, and reduces end-of-day stiffness.

## Duration

- **Typical length:** Ongoing.
- **Frequency:** 5-7 days per week.
- **Session length:** 8-15 minutes.
- **Review interval:** Every 4 weeks.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Exercise Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Mobility and control | Thoracic mobility, wall slide, core |
| Tuesday | Scapular endurance | Mobility, serratus, pull-apart |
| Wednesday | Mobility reset | Thoracic extension and rotation |
| Thursday | Mobility and control | Thoracic mobility, wall slide, core |
| Friday | Scapular endurance | Mobility, serratus, pull-apart |
| Saturday | Optional | Short reset or full routine |
| Sunday | Optional | Rest or mobility only |

## Exercise Order And Dosage

| Order | Exercise | Sets | Reps or Time | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Thoracic Extension](../../exercise-library/mobility/thoracic-extension.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps | 20-30 seconds |
| 2 | [Thoracic Rotation](../../exercise-library/mobility/thoracic-rotation.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps per side | 20-30 seconds |
| 3 | [Wall Slide](../../exercise-library/scapula/wall-slide.md) | 2 | 8-10 reps | 30-45 seconds |
| 4 | [Serratus Punch](../../exercise-library/scapula/serratus-punch.md) | 2 | 10-15 reps | 30-45 seconds |
| 5 | [Dead Bug](../../exercise-library/core/dead-bug.md) | 2 | 6-10 reps per side | 30-45 seconds |

## Progression Rules

Progress when:

- The routine feels easy and improves stiffness within the session.
- Wall slides stay smooth without shrugging.
- Daily symptoms are stable for two weeks.

Progress by adding one set to wall slides or serratus punch before adding harder exercises.

## Regression Rules

Regress when:

- Reduce range on wall slides if neck tension increases.
- Use thoracic mobility only on high-irritability days.
- Remove core work temporarily if it increases shoulder or neck guarding.

## Clinical Checkpoints

- **Pain:** No sharp pain or worsening after the routine.
- **Mobility:** Thoracic stiffness should feel better or unchanged, not worse.
- **Control:** Neck and upper-trapezius tension should not dominate.
- **Habit:** At least 5 sessions completed per week.

## Completion Criteria

This is a maintenance-style program. It is successful when the person can complete it most days and reports better movement awareness, less stiffness, and no symptom escalation over 4 weeks.
