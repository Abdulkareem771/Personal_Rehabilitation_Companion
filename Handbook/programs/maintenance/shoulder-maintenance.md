# Shoulder Maintenance

## Objective

Maintain rotator cuff strength, scapular endurance, thoracic mobility, and trunk support after completion of the shoulder instability program.

## Duration

- **Typical length:** Ongoing.
- **Frequency:** 2 sessions per week.
- **Session length:** 25-40 minutes.
- **Review interval:** Every 4-8 weeks.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Exercise Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Strength maintenance | Mobility, cuff primer, row, scapular accessory, core |
| Day 2 | Optional posture reset | Thoracic mobility and wall slide |
| Day 3 | Rest or sport | No required rehab work |
| Day 4 | Strength maintenance | Mobility, pulldown or row, face pull, core |
| Day 5 | Rest | Symptom check |
| Day 6 | Sport or training | Normal activity if symptoms are stable |
| Day 7 | Rest | Weekly review |

## Exercise Order And Dosage

| Order | Exercise | Sets | Reps or Time | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Thoracic Extension](../../exercise-library/mobility/thoracic-extension.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps | 30 seconds |
| 2 | [Band External Rotation](../../exercise-library/rotator-cuff/band-external-rotation.md) | 2 | 12-15 reps | 45 seconds |
| 3 | [Chest Supported Row](../../exercise-library/gym/chest-supported-row.md) | 3 | 8-12 reps | 90 seconds |
| 4 | [Lat Pulldown Neutral](../../exercise-library/gym/lat-pulldown-neutral.md) | 2-3 | 8-12 reps | 90 seconds |
| 5 | [Cable Face Pull](../../exercise-library/gym/cable-face-pull.md) | 2-3 | 10-15 reps | 60 seconds |
| 6 | [Side Plank](../../exercise-library/core/side-plank.md) | 2 | 20-45 seconds per side | 60 seconds |

## Progression Rules

Progress when:

- Sessions feel routine and do not affect the next day.
- Strength work remains symmetrical and controlled.
- Sport or gym activity is stable for 4 consecutive weeks.

Progress by increasing external training load gradually while keeping this maintenance dose stable.

## Regression Rules

Regress when:

- Return to [Phase 3: Functional Strength](../shoulder-instability/phase-3-functional-strength.md) if gym work creates repeated symptoms.
- Return to [Phase 2: Strength](../shoulder-instability/phase-2-strength.md) if cuff or scapular endurance feels clearly reduced.
- Return to [Phase 1: Stabilization](../shoulder-instability/phase-1-stabilization.md) after a flare with resting pain, apprehension, or daily-activity limitation.

## Clinical Checkpoints

- **Pain:** Stable or absent during normal training weeks.
- **Control:** Pulling and cuff work remain smooth under fatigue.
- **Confidence:** No avoidance of previously cleared activities.
- **Recovery:** No accumulation of symptoms across the week.

## Completion Criteria

Maintenance is ongoing. It is effective when shoulder symptoms remain stable, normal activity continues, and the person can complete two weekly sessions without feeling dependent on daily rehabilitation work.
