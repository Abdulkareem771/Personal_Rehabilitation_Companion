# Phase 3: Functional Strength

## Objective

Integrate rotator cuff strength with scapular control, trunk stability, and pulling strength so the shoulder can tolerate real-world loading.

## Duration

- **Typical length:** 3-6 weeks.
- **Frequency:** 3 strength sessions per week.
- **Session length:** 35-45 minutes.
- **Progression gate:** Strength work remains controlled under moderate fatigue.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Exercise Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Pulling strength | Mobility, cuff primer, row, scapular accessory |
| Day 2 | Rest or lower body | No heavy shoulder loading |
| Day 3 | Scapular endurance | Mobility, prone work, face pull, core |
| Day 4 | Recovery | Posture and thoracic mobility |
| Day 5 | Integrated strength | Cuff, row or pulldown, trunk, lower body |
| Day 6 | Optional easy mobility | Symptom-led |
| Day 7 | Rest | Readiness review |

## Exercise Order And Dosage

| Order | Exercise | Sets | Reps or Time | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Thoracic Extension](../../exercise-library/mobility/thoracic-extension.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps | 30 seconds |
| 2 | [Band External Rotation](../../exercise-library/rotator-cuff/band-external-rotation.md) | 2 | 12-15 reps | 45 seconds |
| 3 | [Chest Supported Row](../../exercise-library/gym/chest-supported-row.md) | 3-4 | 8-12 reps | 75-120 seconds |
| 4 | [Face Pull](../../exercise-library/scapula/face-pull.md) | 3 | 10-15 reps | 60-75 seconds |
| 5 | [Prone Y](../../exercise-library/scapula/prone-y.md) | 2-3 | 8-12 reps | 60 seconds |
| 6 | [Prone T](../../exercise-library/scapula/prone-t.md) | 2-3 | 8-12 reps | 60 seconds |
| 7 | [Side Plank](../../exercise-library/core/side-plank.md) | 2-3 | 15-30 seconds per side | 60 seconds |
| 8 | [Goblet Squat](../../exercise-library/lower-body/goblet-squat.md) | 2-3 | 8-12 reps | 75 seconds |

## Progression Rules

Progress when:

- Rowing volume increases without shoulder-front discomfort.
- Face pulls remain smooth without lumbar extension or neck tension.
- Prone Y and prone T are completed without shrugging.
- Side plank position does not provoke shoulder apprehension.

Progress to Phase 4 when:

- Moderate pulling loads are tolerated for repeated sets.
- The person can train on nonconsecutive days without accumulating symptoms.
- Shoulder confidence is present during loaded tasks at and below shoulder height.

## Regression Rules

Regress when:

- Substitute [Band Pull Apart](../../exercise-library/scapula/band-pull-apart.md) for face pulls if cable or band angle is provocative.
- Reduce row load and keep chest support if trunk compensation appears.
- Return to Phase 2 cuff volume if shoulder symptoms rise after compound work.
- Replace side plank with [Dead Bug](../../exercise-library/core/dead-bug.md) if weight-bearing through the shoulder is not tolerated.

## Clinical Checkpoints

- **Pain:** No sharp anterior shoulder pain during pulling.
- **Control:** Scapular upward rotation and retraction remain coordinated.
- **Strength:** Pulling work feels muscular, not unstable.
- **Recovery:** Symptoms return to baseline within 24 hours.
- **Function:** Routine gym, work, and household loading are more confident than in Phase 2.

## Completion Criteria

Phase 3 is complete when the person can complete three integrated strength sessions in one week with stable symptoms, controlled fatigue, and no avoidance of normal daily loading.
