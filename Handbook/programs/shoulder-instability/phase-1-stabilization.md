# Phase 1: Stabilization

## Objective

Restore comfortable movement, reduce protective guarding, and establish low-load rotator cuff and scapular control.

## Duration

- **Typical length:** 2-4 weeks.
- **Frequency:** 4-6 short sessions per week.
- **Session length:** 15-25 minutes.
- **Progression gate:** At least 7 consecutive days without next-day symptom increase.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Exercise Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Control session | Mobility, isometrics, scapular activation |
| Day 2 | Recovery session | Mobility and posture reset |
| Day 3 | Control session | Mobility, isometrics, scapular activation |
| Day 4 | Rest or walking | No loaded shoulder work |
| Day 5 | Control session | Mobility, isometrics, scapular activation |
| Day 6 | Recovery session | Mobility and easy trunk control |
| Day 7 | Rest | Symptom review |

## Exercise Order And Dosage

| Order | Exercise | Sets | Reps or Time | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Pendulum](../../exercise-library/mobility/pendulum.md) | 2 | 30-45 seconds | 30 seconds |
| 2 | [Thoracic Extension](../../exercise-library/mobility/thoracic-extension.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps | 30 seconds |
| 3 | [Isometric External Rotation](../../exercise-library/rotator-cuff/isometric-external-rotation.md) | 3 | 10-20 second holds | 30-45 seconds |
| 4 | [Isometric Internal Rotation](../../exercise-library/rotator-cuff/isometric-internal-rotation.md) | 3 | 10-20 second holds | 30-45 seconds |
| 5 | [Serratus Punch](../../exercise-library/scapula/serratus-punch.md) | 2-3 | 8-12 reps | 45 seconds |
| 6 | [Wall Slide](../../exercise-library/scapula/wall-slide.md) | 2 | 6-10 reps | 45 seconds |
| 7 | [Dead Bug](../../exercise-library/core/dead-bug.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps per side | 45 seconds |

## Progression Rules

Progress to Phase 2 when all criteria are met:

- Isometrics are tolerated at moderate effort without apprehension.
- Wall slides are smooth without shoulder hiking.
- Daily reaching below shoulder height is comfortable.
- No next-day increase in pain, heaviness, or instability after three consecutive control sessions.

## Regression Rules

Regress within this phase when any criterion is present:

- Replace wall slides with fewer reps or a shorter range if upper-trapezius dominance appears.
- Reduce isometric effort or hold time if pain rises above 2/10.
- Use pendulum and thoracic mobility only for 24-48 hours if symptoms flare.
- Remove trunk work temporarily if bracing increases shoulder tension.

## Clinical Checkpoints

- **Pain:** 0-2/10 during session and baseline by the next morning.
- **Control:** Scapula moves without winging or shrugging during low-range work.
- **Range:** Comfortable daily range below shoulder height.
- **Confidence:** No sense of slipping during unloaded movements.

## Completion Criteria

Phase 1 is complete when the person can complete the full listed session on three separate days with stable symptoms and visible scapular control.
