# Phase 2: Strength

## Objective

Build rotator cuff capacity and scapular endurance using controlled resistance while preserving shoulder confidence.

## Duration

- **Typical length:** 3-5 weeks.
- **Frequency:** 3-4 strength sessions per week.
- **Session length:** 25-35 minutes.
- **Progression gate:** Complete the final week without form breakdown or next-day symptom increase.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Focus | Exercise Order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Cuff strength | Mobility, external/internal rotation, serratus |
| Day 2 | Recovery | Thoracic mobility and easy posture work |
| Day 3 | Scapular strength | Mobility, wall slide, pull-apart, prone work |
| Day 4 | Rest | Symptom review |
| Day 5 | Combined strength | Cuff, scapula, core |
| Day 6 | Optional recovery | Mobility only |
| Day 7 | Rest | Readiness check |

## Exercise Order And Dosage

| Order | Exercise | Sets | Reps or Time | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Thoracic Extension](../../exercise-library/mobility/thoracic-extension.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps | 30 seconds |
| 2 | [Thoracic Rotation](../../exercise-library/mobility/thoracic-rotation.md) | 2 | 6-8 reps per side | 30 seconds |
| 3 | [Band External Rotation](../../exercise-library/rotator-cuff/band-external-rotation.md) | 3 | 10-15 reps | 45-60 seconds |
| 4 | [Band Internal Rotation](../../exercise-library/rotator-cuff/band-internal-rotation.md) | 3 | 10-15 reps | 45-60 seconds |
| 5 | [Side Lying External Rotation](../../exercise-library/rotator-cuff/side-lying-external-rotation.md) | 2-3 | 8-12 reps | 60 seconds |
| 6 | [Serratus Punch](../../exercise-library/scapula/serratus-punch.md) | 3 | 10-15 reps | 45 seconds |
| 7 | [Band Pull Apart](../../exercise-library/scapula/band-pull-apart.md) | 2-3 | 10-15 reps | 60 seconds |
| 8 | [Bird Dog](../../exercise-library/core/bird-dog.md) | 2 | 6-10 reps per side | 45 seconds |

## Progression Rules

Progress load or volume when:

- All prescribed reps are completed with steady scapular position.
- The final 2 reps feel effortful but controlled.
- Symptoms remain 0-2/10 and settle by the next morning.
- Resistance can be increased without changing range, tempo, or posture.

Progress to Phase 3 when:

- Band external and internal rotation are tolerated for 3 sets of 15.
- Pull-aparts and serratus work do not create neck dominance.
- The shoulder feels stable during routine lifting and reaching.

## Regression Rules

Regress when:

- Reduce band resistance before reducing exercise selection.
- Drop from 3 sets to 2 sets if fatigue changes shoulder position.
- Return to isometrics from Phase 1 if rotation work causes apprehension.
- Remove side-lying external rotation temporarily if symptoms localize sharply at the shoulder.

## Clinical Checkpoints

- **Pain:** No progressive increase across the session.
- **Control:** Humeral rotation occurs without trunk twisting.
- **Endurance:** Scapular position remains steady across repeated sets.
- **Daily function:** Dressing, desk work, and light household tasks are not worse after training days.

## Completion Criteria

Phase 2 is complete when the person tolerates three weekly strengthening sessions for two consecutive weeks with controlled form and no next-day symptom escalation.
