# Instability Signs

Instability signs help identify when a joint may not be tolerating the current position, load, or speed.

This document is especially relevant for shoulder instability programs.

## Common Signs

Watch for:

- Apprehension.
- Sensation of slipping or shifting.
- Sudden protective guarding.
- Loss of confidence in a position.
- Repeated catching or clunking with symptoms.
- Avoidance of a range that previously felt safe.
- Symptoms that feel different from muscular effort.

## During Exercise

If instability signs appear:

- Stop the current set.
- Reduce range or load.
- Use a supported position.
- Return to an earlier phase if signs repeat.
- Do not use fatigue to test the joint.

## After Exercise

Instability-related symptoms may show up as:

- Increased guarding later that day.
- Avoidance of daily reaching.
- Feeling loose, unsafe, or unreliable.
- Sleep disturbance due to shoulder position.

## Clinical Review Triggers

Clinical review is appropriate if:

- A dislocation or subluxation is suspected.
- Instability events repeat.
- Apprehension is increasing.
- Strength is declining.
- Neurological symptoms are present.
