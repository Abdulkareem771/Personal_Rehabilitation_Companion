# Stop Rules

Stop rules define when an exercise, session, or program block should end.

These rules protect against pushing through symptoms that should be reassessed.

## Stop The Exercise

Stop the current exercise if:

- Pain reaches 5/10 or higher.
- Sharp pain occurs.
- Apprehension or instability appears.
- The person cannot maintain control after reducing difficulty.
- Symptoms increase with each repetition.
- Dizziness, numbness, tingling, or unusual weakness appears.

## Stop The Session

Stop the full session if:

- Symptoms do not settle after removing the provocative exercise.
- Multiple exercises provoke the same symptom.
- Movement quality is globally poor due to fatigue.
- The person feels unsafe continuing.
- A new injury occurs.

## Seek Clinical Review

Clinical review is appropriate when:

- Symptoms are worsening week to week.
- Night pain is new or increasing.
- Instability events repeat.
- Neurological symptoms persist.
- Daily function is declining despite regression.

## Return After Stopping

After stopping:

- Record the exercise, load, range, and symptom response.
- Resume with a lower-irritability option only after symptoms settle.
- Use the [flare-up management](./flare-up-management.md) plan if symptoms remain elevated.
